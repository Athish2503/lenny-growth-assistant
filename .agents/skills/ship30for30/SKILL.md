# Ship30for30 Atomic Essay Generation Skill

This skill provides precise guidelines and formatting patterns for synthesizing complex product management, growth, and startup topics into compelling **Ship 30 for 30** style atomic essays.

## Essay Structure Framework

### 1. Single-Idea Headline (Title)
- Specific, benefit-driven, and clear.
- Mentions the core topic, target outcome, or actionable framework.
- Example: *The 4-Step Product-Led Growth Playbook for B2B SaaS Scaleups*

### 2. The Hook (First 1-2 Lines)
- Open with a bold, provocative statement, myth buster, or pattern interrupt.
- Create instant curiosity and state why the traditional approach fails.

### 3. Thesis & Context (Paragraph 1)
- State the core thesis directly without fluff or filler words.
- Explain *why* this problem matters now and what separates top growth teams from the rest.

### 4. Comprehensive Core Framework (~1250 Words Target Depth)
- Divide the core content into 3 to 5 clear sub-sections with bold markdown headers (`###`).
- For each sub-section:
  - **Bold Lead-ins:** Start every bullet point or numbered item with bold text summarizing the core insight.
  - **Skimmable Layout:** Keep paragraphs short (1–3 sentences max) with ample white space.
  - **Actionable Steps:** Provide practical steps, real-world examples, metric targets, and execution tips derived from growth leaders.

### 5. Clear Takeaway & Actionable Summary
- End with a standalone, highly memorable summary block.
- Provide a 1-sentence golden rule or immediate action item the reader can execute today.

## Tone & Style Guidelines
- **Punchy & Direct:** Active voice, conversational yet authoritative.
- **Visual Skimmability:** Use bullet points, numbered lists, bold text for key terms, and short sections.
- **Zero Fluff:** Every sentence must deliver practical value.
